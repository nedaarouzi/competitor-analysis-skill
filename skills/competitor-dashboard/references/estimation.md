# Estimation guide

Start from the category ranges in `benchmarks.json`, then adjust for company size. Always give a range and a confidence level. Estimates built from public ad libraries show direction, not truth.

## Scaling the budget

| Signal | Multiply the midpoint by |
|---|---|
| Free or freemium pricing | 0.6 to 0.8 |
| Subscription of £50 a month or more | 1.2 to 1.5 |
| VC-backed, Series A or later | 1.5 to 2.0 |
| Bootstrapped or small team | 0.5 to 0.7 |
| Large national brand (TV advertiser, £10m or more revenue) | Well above the ranges. Say so. |

Also use the number of active ads and how long they have run in the ad libraries. Many ads that have run for months suggest steady spend.

## Scaling CPL by channel maturity

| Situation | CPL versus benchmark |
|---|---|
| Brand-new channel for the focal company | 1.5 to 2 times |
| Optimised channel, 6 months or more | 0.7 to 0.9 times |

## Calibration

If the person gives one real number (for example their own CPL or monthly spend), mark it verified, compare each competitor with it, and adjust. Say which number you used and how far the estimates moved.

## CPL colour rules (Tab 4, focal company)

Green: at or below the benchmark low. Gold: between the low and twice the benchmark midpoint. Red: twice the benchmark midpoint or higher. Competitor CPL cells are green when the channel is active.
